Neurodivergent Community Living

Concept site & house plans · pocket-neighborhood cluster

📍 New Jersey · DDD path 🧩 High / behavioral support 🏠 4 houses · 12 residents each 👥 48 residents total 🛏️ All private ensuite rooms ⚧ Single-gender pods

1 · Site plan — four houses around a shared commons

Homes face inward onto a car-free green. Parking & service sit at the edges; one secure landscaped perimeter wraps the whole campus so residents can roam the interior freely.

Resident house Community building Commons green Sensory garden Parking (edge)
Why inward-facing? Porches onto the green create a graduated social space — a resident can watch from the porch, step onto the green, or stay inside. Houses also see each other, giving natural "eyes on the green" without anyone feeling surveilled.

2 · House plan — the two-pod model

Each house holds 12 residents, split into two calm single-gender households (pods) of 6 — joined by a shared central core, wrapping a secure private rear courtyard. Every bedroom has its own ensuite bath. Single-story.

Pod A Pod B Shared great room Staff hub Quiet / sensory 🚿 Private ensuite Rear courtyard
The pod split is the key move: it gives you the capacity of a big building with the calm, family scale high-support residents need — and it maps cleanly onto NJ small-household licensing. Private bedrooms + per-pod quiet rooms mean every resident can always retreat without crossing a crowd.
Concept design only — not architectural, engineering, or legal advice.
