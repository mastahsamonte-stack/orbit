# HCS / IDD Provider Acquisition — Due Diligence Kit

**Target market:** San Antonio / Bexar County, Texas
**Use case:** Buying an existing HCS (Home and Community-based Services) provider — and/or an HCSSA personal-assistance agency — to acquire its enrolled client census, DSP workforce, and Medicaid contract.

> **Why this kit matters:** In Texas the scarce asset is *enrolled clients*, not the contract. HHSC froze new HCS interest-list slot releases (May 2026 onward) and the HCS waitlist is ~68,000 people. So when you buy, you are really buying (1) a transferable census of authorized individuals, (2) a clean Medicaid/HCS contract that will actually assign to you, and (3) a trained DSP workforce that won't walk. Every item below is built to pressure-test those three things.

---

## 0. How to use this document

1. Send **Section 1 (Document Request List)** to the seller / broker the day you sign an NDA or LOI.
2. Work **Sections 2–9** as you receive documents — each has *what to verify* and *red flags / deal-killers*.
3. Use **Section 10** to structure the purchase agreement so closing is contingent on HHSC's written approval of the contract assignment.
4. Track everything in the companion **Deal-Sourcing & Diligence Tracker** (CSV).

Engage a **Texas healthcare M&A attorney** and a **CPA** before money moves. This kit organizes the work; it does not replace counsel.

## 1. Initial document request list (send to seller on LOI)

**Corporate & ownership**
- [ ] Certificate of Formation, operating agreement, org chart, cap table
- [ ] List of all owners / controlling persons (for HHSC disclosure Form 5871)
- [ ] Any prior change-of-ownership history

**Licenses & contracts**
- [ ] HCS provider contract + component code(s), contract number, effective dates
- [ ] HCSSA license(s) and category (PAS / Licensed Home Health / etc.)
- [ ] TxHmL contract if applicable
- [ ] Medicaid enrollment record (TMHP/PEMS) and NPI(s)
- [ ] MCO contracts (Superior, Molina, UnitedHealthcare) if doing STAR+PLUS
- [ ] Any DFPS SIL contract if serving foster youth

**Regulatory / compliance**
- [ ] Last 3 years of HHSC survey reports, statements of deficiency, plans of correction
- [ ] Any sanctions, vendor holds, payment holds, corrective action, or contract-termination notices
- [ ] Incident / Abuse-Neglect-Exploitation (ANE) reports filed (last 3 years)
- [ ] EVV compliance reports / visit-rejection rates (HHAeXchange)

**Clients / census**
- [ ] Monthly enrolled census by program, last 24–36 months
- [ ] Census by service type (residential, day hab, supported home living, respite, supported employment)
- [ ] Group/host-home roster with addresses, capacity, occupancy
- [ ] LIDDA (AACOG) relationship history — referral volume

**Financials**
- [ ] 3 years P&L, balance sheet, tax returns
- [ ] Trailing-12-month monthly P&L
- [ ] AR aging report (by payer)
- [ ] Billing/claims data: submitted, paid, denied, recouped (last 24 months)
- [ ] Debt schedule, leases (offices + each residence)

**Workforce**
- [ ] Full staff roster: role, hire date, wage, status (W-2/1099), FT/PT
- [ ] DSP turnover data (last 24 months)
- [ ] Administrator + Alternate Administrator credentials & training records
- [ ] Background-check, EMR, NAR, and training files (sample + completeness audit)
- [ ] Open positions / current vacancies

## 2. The census — your #1 asset (and #1 fraud risk)

**What to verify**
- Reconcile the **enrolled-individual count** against HHSC service authorizations (SASO) — not just the seller's spreadsheet.
- Trend monthly census over **24–36 months**. Flat or growing = good. Declining = price it down hard even if EBITDA looks fine.
- Confirm each individual's **slot is active and authorized**, with current Individual Plan of Care (IPC) / Person-Directed Plan (PDP).
- Understand concentration: how many individuals, in how many homes, referred by which LIDDA contacts.

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 Census claimed ≠ census in HHSC authorizations.
- 🚩 Several individuals whose plans are lapsed / pending renewal (revenue at risk).
- 🚩 Heavy reliance on a handful of individuals or one referral source.
- 🚩 Recent disenrollments or families signaling they'll switch providers post-sale (interview retention risk).

---

## 3. The contract & its assignability — does the deal even work?

**What to verify**
- The HCS contract is in **good standing**, no vendor hold, no termination/non-renewal notice.
- Confirm the contract is **assignable** via HHSC's change-of-ownership / contract-assignment process.
- Map the **two parallel approvals**: (a) HCSSA license CHOW via TULIP (file ≥30 days before close, Form 1092); (b) Medicaid/HCS **contract assignment** (assignor notifies HHSC ≥60 days before; **no assignment effective until HHSC approves in writing**).
- Confirm service authorizations will mass-transfer to your entity on approval (via SASO).

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 Any open HHSC enforcement, payment hold, or pending contract action — these can block assignment.
- 🚩 Seller can't produce the contract or component code.
- 🚩 Owner has a prior sanction/termination in last 5 years → triggers a **$50,000 Medicaid surety bond** for the receiving entity.
- 🚩 Structuring as an **asset purchase** when the contract only transfers via **equity/assignment** — get this right with counsel; the wrong structure can leave you with no clients on day one.

## 4. Survey & compliance history

**What to verify**
- Pull the seller's record yourself from the **HHSC Long-Term Care Provider Search** (apps.hhs.texas.gov/LTCSearch) — don't rely only on what they hand you.
- Read every **Statement of Deficiency + Plan of Correction** for the last 3 years. Look for *repeat* deficiencies (systemic) vs. one-offs.
- Check for ANE findings, life-safety/physical-plant citations on residences.

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 Repeated or unresolved deficiencies; pattern of late plans of correction.
- 🚩 Any confirmed abuse/neglect/exploitation finding tied to current staff.
- 🚩 Citations that imply expensive remediation (home modifications, staffing-ratio failures).

---

## 5. Billing, claims & revenue integrity

**What to verify**
- Recompute revenue from **claims data**, not just the P&L. Match billed → paid → denied → recouped.
- Denial rate and top denial reasons (EVV mismatches are common and fixable; medical-necessity/eligibility denials are worse).
- **EVV compliance**: visit-rejection rate via HHAeXchange; visits submitted within the 60-day window.
- Rate basis: confirm reimbursement against current **HHSC Provider Finance rate tables** (post-Sept-2025 restructure: ~$13.00/hr average attendant wage, 14–15% payroll-tax-and-benefits allowance). Margin upside is overhead/scale, **not** rate negotiation.

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 High denial/recoupment rate, or a spike before sale (possible aggressive billing to inflate revenue).
- 🚩 EVV non-compliance → claims at risk + audit exposure you inherit.
- 🚩 Revenue concentrated in one service line that's being rate-cut.

---

## 6. Accounts receivable & working capital

**What to verify**
- AR aging by payer; collectibility of >90-day balances.
- **Payroll float**: the gap between weekly DSP payroll and 30–90-day Medicaid/MCO collection. Size the working capital you must inject day one.
- Whether existing factoring/lines are in place and their cost (~18–36% annualized if factoring).

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 Large aged AR the seller is counting as value but won't collect.
- 🚩 No working-capital cushion — you'd fund several months of payroll before claims pay out.

---

## 7. Workforce & DSP retention — the operational moat

**What to verify**
- DSP **turnover** vs. industry median (~75% in 2024). Lower = real value.
- Wage competitiveness vs. the ~$13/hr attendant rate basis (underpaying = post-close exodus risk).
- Administrator + **Alternate Administrator** in place and properly trained (24-hr initial + 12-hr CADE). Losing the administrator can stall operations.
- Completeness audit of background checks (DPS Ch. 250), **EMR**, **NAR**, ANE training (≥80% scores), CPR/First Aid.
- Interview key staff (under NDA) about whether they'll stay post-sale.

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 Turnover well above median, or several open DSP roles propping up census on overtime.
- 🚩 Missing/expired background or registry checks (inherited liability + survey risk).
- 🚩 Administrator leaving with the seller and no qualified replacement lined up.

---

## 8. Real estate / residences (host & group homes)

**What to verify**
- Each residence: owned vs. leased, lease assignability, term, rent vs. market.
- Zoning / certificate of occupancy / fire-marshal clearance for the bed count.
- Physical-plant condition and any deferred capital (sprinklers, accessibility).

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 Leases not assignable or expiring soon.
- 🚩 Residence out of compliance with life-safety code (expensive to cure).

---

## 9. Legal, tax & liabilities

**What to verify**
- Litigation, employment claims, wage/hour (misclassification of DSPs as 1099 is a classic landmine).
- Tax liens, unpaid payroll taxes, unemployment claims.
- Outstanding HHSC recoupments or audit findings.

**Red flags / deal-killers**
- 🚩 1099-misclassified caregivers → back-tax + penalty exposure that follows the business.
- 🚩 Open Medicaid audit / recoupment demand.

---

## 10. Deal structure & closing mechanics

**Build these into the LOI / purchase agreement:**
- **Closing contingent on HHSC's written approval of the contract assignment** — non-negotiable. No approval, no close.
- Align timeline to the **60-day contract-assignment notice + HHSC approval** (this drives the calendar) and the **30-day HCSSA CHOW filing** via TULIP.
- **Holdback / escrow** (e.g., 10–20%) released after: (a) HHSC approval, (b) census reconciliation post-transfer, (c) clean trailing-90-day claims with no surprise recoupments.
- **Reps & warranties** on: census accuracy, contract good standing, no undisclosed surveys/sanctions, complete background/registry files, no 1099 misclassification, AR collectibility.
- **Indemnification** for pre-close Medicaid recoupments, ANE findings, and employment claims.
- **Transition services agreement**: seller + administrator stay 60–90 days to hold the LIDDA relationship and reassure families/staff.
- Confirm whether to structure as **stock/equity purchase** (contract rides along) vs. **asset purchase** (contract must be re-assigned) — counsel decides based on how the HCS contract transfers cleanly.
