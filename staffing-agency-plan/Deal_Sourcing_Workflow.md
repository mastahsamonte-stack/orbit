# Deal-Sourcing Workflow — Bexar County DSP / HCS / Home-Care Acquisitions

A repeatable process to build, rank, and work a pipeline of acquisition targets in San Antonio / Bexar County. Pair this with the **Deal_Sourcing_Target_List.csv** tracker.

---

## Channel 1 — Direct outreach via the HHSC provider database (highest-leverage)

This is where you find owners who aren't formally "for sale" yet — often the best deals (retirements, burnout, no succession plan).

**Step 1 — Pull the list.** Go to the **HHSC Long-Term Care Provider Search**: https://apps.hhs.texas.gov/LTCSearch/index.cfm
- Filter by **county = Bexar** (and nearby: Comal, Guadalupe, Medina, Atascosa for a wider net).
- Filter by program type: **HCS**, **TxHmL**, and **HCSSA (PAS / Home Health)**.
- The results list owner name, services, license/contract, number served, and compliance history.

**Step 2 — Capture into the tracker.** For each provider, fill a row in `Deal_Sourcing_Target_List.csv`: name, program, # served, owner, location, years licensed, any visible compliance flags.

**Step 3 — Score each target** (the CSV has a column; scoring rubric below).

**Step 4 — Cross-check compliance** for your top 20 in the same database — pull survey/deficiency history before you spend outreach effort.

**Step 5 — Outreach.** Owner-direct letter/call. Lead with discretion and succession ("Are you thinking about your next chapter?"), not a lowball. Track touches/dates in the tracker.

### Target scoring rubric (0–5 each; higher = more attractive)
| Factor | 5 = great | 1 = poor |
|---|---|---|
| Census size & trend | Growing, 20+ served | Tiny / shrinking |
| Compliance history | Clean, no repeat deficiencies | Sanctions / repeat findings |
| Owner motivation | Retiring, no successor | No reason to sell |
| Contract type | HCS w/ residential census | Thin single-service |
| Payer/referral mix | Diversified, strong LIDDA ties | One referral source |
| Workforce stability | Low turnover, admin in place | High turnover, no alternate admin |

Sum the scores in the tracker to rank the pipeline.

## Channel 2 — Business-for-sale marketplaces (monitor weekly)

Set saved searches; filter keywords **"HCS," "IDD," "Medicaid waiver," "home and community based"** to separate IDD providers from generic home health.

- BizBuySell — San Antonio: https://www.bizbuysell.com/texas/san-antonio-metro-area/home-health-care-businesses-for-sale/

## Channel 3 — Healthcare-specialized M&A brokers (engage for the deal)

- **VERTESS** — DFW-based, IDD/autism is a named specialty; most relevant for an HCS target. https://vertess.com/service/intellectualdevelopmental-disabilities-and-autism-services
- **Stoneridge Partners** — national home care/health/hospice M&A, strong TX roots. https://www.stoneridgepartners.com/agencies-for-sale/
- **The Braff Group** — sell-side home health/home care/hospice. https://thebraffgroup.com/market-sectors/home-health-hospice/
- **Home Care Business Broker** — https://www.homecarebusinessbroker.com/
- **Transworld of South & West Texas** (local, smaller targets) — https://transworldsanantonio.com/
- ⚠️ The "Tenney Group" in search results is a *transportation* M&A firm — not relevant here.

---

## Channel 4 — Relationship / referral sourcing

- **AACOG** (Bexar County LIDDA) — the referral gatekeeper for HCS/TxHmL; build this relationship early (intake 210-832-5020). They know which providers are struggling or exiting.
- **TAHC&H** (Texas Association for Home Care & Hospice) — 1,200+ member agencies; conferences and member directory surface sellers. https://tahch.org/home
- STAR+PLUS MCO provider-relations reps (Superior, Molina, UHC) hear about agencies winding down.

---

## Suggested cadence (first 90 days)
- **Weeks 1–2:** Pull HHSC list, build tracker, score top 30.
- **Weeks 2–4:** Compliance cross-check top 20; engage VERTESS/Stoneridge; set marketplace alerts.
- **Weeks 3–8:** Outreach wave 1 (top 15 owner-direct); log responses.
- **Weeks 6–12:** NDAs + diligence kit to warm targets; build LOIs on the best 2–3.
