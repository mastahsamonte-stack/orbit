# NON-DISCLOSURE AND WORK-FOR-HIRE AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** ______________________________

**Between:**
**Disclosing Party (Client):** Philip Samonte, The Prime Strategy
**Receiving Party (Contractor/VA):** Kisha Parubrub

---

**1. PURPOSE**
The Contractor is being engaged to build and configure a private deal network system ("the Project") for the Client. In connection with this work, the Client will share proprietary business systems, designs, workflows, and strategies. This Agreement protects that information.

---

**2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**
Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:
- Business system design, structure, and workflows
- Branding, naming, terminology, and concepts related to the Project
- CRM configuration, automations, forms, and pipelines built for the Client
- Pricing, fee structures, and business model details
- Member lists, contact data, and deal information
- Any materials, files, or documents shared by the Client

---

**3. CONTRACTOR'S OBLIGATIONS**
The Contractor agrees to:
- Keep all Confidential Information strictly private
- Not disclose, share, or discuss the Project with any third party
- Not use Confidential Information for any purpose outside this engagement
- Not build a substantially similar system for any competing client in the real estate investing, creative financing, or lead network space for **24 months** from the date of this Agreement
- Delete or return all Client materials upon request or project completion

---

**4. WORK-FOR-HIRE**
All work product created by the Contractor under this engagement — including but not limited to CRM builds, workflows, automations, forms, documents, and configurations — is considered **work-for-hire** and is the sole and exclusive property of the Client, Philip Samonte / The Prime Strategy. The Contractor retains no rights, ownership, or license to any deliverable produced for this project.

---

**5. THIRD-PARTY SPECIALISTS**
The Contractor may engage third-party specialists (including GHL specialists) to assist with this project. The Contractor is fully responsible for ensuring that any such third party is made aware of and agrees to the confidentiality obligations in this Agreement before any Client information is shared with them. The Contractor shall obtain a signed confidentiality agreement from any third party prior to disclosure.

---

**6. EXCEPTIONS**
These obligations do not apply to information that:
- Was already publicly known before disclosure
- Becomes public through no fault of the Contractor
- Was independently developed by the Contractor without use of Client's information

---

**7. TERM**
This Agreement is effective from the date signed and remains in force for **3 years**, or indefinitely with respect to any trade secrets.

---

**8. REMEDIES**
The Contractor acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Client. The Client shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and/or monetary damages without the requirement of posting a bond.

---

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, United States of America, without regard to its conflict-of-law provisions. The parties acknowledge that the Contractor is located in the Republic of the Philippines, and both parties agree that this Agreement is also enforceable in the Philippines in accordance with applicable Philippine law, including Republic Act No. 8293 (Intellectual Property Code) and Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act).

**9A. INTERNATIONAL ARBITRATION**
Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its breach, termination, or validity, shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration administered under the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) Rules of Arbitration. The arbitration shall be conducted in English, with the seat of arbitration in Boston, Massachusetts, USA. The arbitral award shall be final and binding on both parties. Both parties acknowledge that the Philippines is a signatory to the Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards (New York Convention, 1958), and that any arbitral award rendered hereunder shall be enforceable in the Philippines pursuant to that Convention and Republic Act No. 9285 (Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of 2004).
